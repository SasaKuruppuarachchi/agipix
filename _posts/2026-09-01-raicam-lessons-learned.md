RAICAM is a Marie Skłodowska-Curie doctoral network on robotics and AI for critical asset monitoring. It was designed around a research challenge shared by the whole cohort. This article gathers what the network learned from its research sprints and field demonstrations and turns those lessons into practical guidelines for future doctoral networks.

## How AgiPIX was used

AgiPIX was used in the RAICAM work this article reports on.
